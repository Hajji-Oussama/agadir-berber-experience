## Do You Need a Driving License for a Buggy in Morocco?

Yes. The driver of the buggy must show a valid physical car driving license before departure.

Reason is mechanical, not administrative:

- A quad uses handlebars and thumb throttle, classified as easy automatic leisure ATV for guided tours
- A buggy uses a car-style steering wheel, foot brake and accelerator pedal, with much higher torque and weight
- Moroccan regulations treat the buggy as a motorized terrestrial vehicle requiring standard driving skills

Practical rules:

- Driver: standard car license (B) required, photo or photocopy usually accepted but bring the original
- Passenger: no license needed, can be child or adult
- You can swap driver mid-tour if both have licenses
- Quad alternative: if no one has a license, book [Quad Biking](/en/experiences/quad-biking) instead, no license required

## The Circuit: Dunes, Ocean Trails and Berber Tea Break

The 2-hour buggy circuit from Agadir is not the Sahara. It is the coastal adventure belt of Aghroud, 15 minutes from Agadir, near Kasbat Souss.

What you actually ride through:

1. **Aghroud sand dunes** – rolling coastal dunes for acceleration and turns
2. **Ocean trails** – dirt tracks with Atlantic views towards Taghazout
3. **Eucalyptus forest** – shaded technical section, cooler and dusty
4. **Berber village stop** – 20-minute mint tea break with local hosts, use of traditional tents, photos in Amazigh scarves

## FAQ: Buggy in Agadir

### What is the real price of a buggy tour in Agadir?

### Do I need a driving license for a buggy in Morocco?

Yes, the driver needs a valid car driving license. The buggy drives with a steering wheel and pedals like a car, unlike automatic quads which need no license. Passengers do not need any license.

### Is hotel pickup included from Agadir and Taghazout?

Yes, free round-trip hotel pickup is included from all hotels, apartments and surf camps in Agadir, Taghazout and Tamraght. Just send your hotel name and location pin when booking.

### Can children ride as passengers in a buggy?

Yes. Children from 4-5 years old can ride as passengers next to an adult driver, with helmet and secured seatbelt. They cannot drive. For families, the buggy is safer and more comfortable than a separate quad thanks to the roll cage and bucket seats.
