Your immersive experience begins at Kasbah Souss (Bensergaw Traditional Crafts Complex), home to our welcoming restaurant. Accompanied by your expert guide, you will head to the local market (Souk) to handpick fresh vegetables and aromatic natural spices while soaking in the vibrant, authentic atmosphere of Moroccan daily life.
 Afterwards, return with your fresh ingredients to our center for an interactive, fun-filled Cooking Class with a professional chef. While your choice of an authentic dish—whether a traditional Tagine, Couscous, or another Moroccan specialty—is simmering to perfection, you'll have free time to explore the hidden gems of the Traditional Crafts Complex and discover the richness of Moroccan and Amazigh heritage.
