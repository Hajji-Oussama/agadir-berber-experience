A Journey into Authentic Moroccan Flavor & Heritage! 🇲🇦
Your immersive experience begins at Kasbah Souss (Bensergaw Traditional Crafts Complex), home to our welcoming restaurant. Accompanied by your expert guide, you head into the local market — the souk — to handpick fresh vegetables and aromatic natural spices while soaking in the vibrant, authentic atmosphere of Moroccan daily life.

Afterwards, return with your fresh ingredients to our centre for an interactive, fun-filled Cooking Class with a professional chef. While your choice of an authentic dish — whether a traditional tagine, couscous, or another Moroccan specialty — is simmering to perfection, you'll have free time to explore the hidden gems of the Traditional Crafts Complex and discover the richness of Moroccan and Amazigh heritage.

The Experience (Itinerary)
1. Hotel Pickup & Welcome at Kasbah Souss. We collect you from your hotel and bring you to the Bensergaw Traditional Crafts Complex, where our restaurant and kitchen await. Mint tea to start, as always.

2. The Souk Walk. Accompanied by your expert guide, you walk into the local market to handpick fresh vegetables and aromatic natural spices — the same stalls locals use every day. Bargaining, colours, and the smell of cumin and coriander in the air.

3. The Cooking Class. Back at our centre, you cook side by side with a professional chef in an interactive, fun-filled session. You choose your dish — traditional tagine, couscous, or another Moroccan specialty — and learn every step from preparation to plating.

4. Free Time in the Crafts Complex. While your dish simmers slowly to perfection, wander Kasbah Souss at your own pace: potters, leatherworkers, silversmiths and weavers, all working by hand. This is where Moroccan and Amazigh heritage lives.

5. The Feast. You sit down and eat what you cooked — with mint tea, conversation, and the satisfaction of a meal you'll be recreating at home for years.

Cultural Connection
Food is the most honest door into Amazigh culture. The dish you'll cook is the same one families across the Souss-Massa region have made for generations — slow, patient, built around what the land and the souk provide. And because your class takes place inside Kasbah Souss, you're surrounded by the other crafts that define this heritage: pottery, leather, silver and weaving, each one a different language of the same culture. You're not just learning a recipe — you're spending a day inside a living tradition.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need any cooking experience?
None at all. Our chef guides you through every step, and the class is designed to be fun for complete beginners and confident cooks alike.

Can I choose which dish to cook?
Yes — you choose between a traditional tagine, couscous, or another Moroccan specialty. Let us know any preferences when you book.

Is it suitable for children and families?
Absolutely — it's one of our most family-friendly experiences, and kids love the souk walk and getting their hands into the cooking. The family coupon makes it even better value.

What if I have dietary restrictions?
Tell us in advance (vegetarian, allergies, etc.) and we'll adapt your ingredients and dish. Moroccan cuisine is naturally flexible.

How long is the whole experience?
About 5 hours from pickup to drop-off, including the souk visit, cooking class, free time in the crafts complex, and the meal.
