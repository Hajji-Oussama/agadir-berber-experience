Overview
Anyone can eat a tagine in Agadir. Very few visitors ever learn how one is actually made — from the spice stall to the simmering pot. This is your chance to do exactly that, in the most authentic way possible: by walking into a real Moroccan souk with a local guide, choosing your own vegetables and spices, and then cooking a proper dish from scratch with a professional chef.

Your journey begins at Kasbah Souss (the Bensergaw Traditional Crafts Complex), where our restaurant and kitchen sit at the heart of a living Amazigh artisan quarter. From there, you and your guide head into the local souk — not a tourist market, but the real thing — to handpick fresh vegetables and aromatic spices while soaking in the noise, colour, and rhythm of daily Moroccan life.

Then it's back to the kitchen. You'll cook side by side with our chef, learning the techniques behind a traditional tagine, couscous, or another Moroccan specialty of your choice. And while your dish simmers slowly to perfection — the way it should — you'll have free time to wander the crafts complex and discover the richness of Moroccan and Amazigh heritage before sitting down to eat what you made.

"We've taken cooking classes in Thailand and Italy, and this was better than both. Buying our own spices in the souk, then cooking with a real chef — it felt like being welcomed into someone's home, not a tourist attraction." — Emma & Lars, Copenhagen, Denmark ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

The Experience (Itinerary)
1. Hotel Pickup & Welcome. We collect you from your hotel and bring you to Kasbah Souss, where our restaurant and kitchen await. Mint tea to start, as always.

2. The Souk Walk. Accompanied by your expert guide, you head into the local market to handpick fresh vegetables and aromatic natural spices. This is where the real Morocco shows itself — bargaining, colours, and the smell of cumin and coriander in the air.

3. The Cooking Class. Back at our centre, you cook with a professional chef in an interactive, fun-filled session. You choose your dish — traditional tagine, couscous, or another Moroccan specialty — and learn every step from preparation to plating.

4. Free Time in the Crafts Complex. While your dish simmers to perfection, explore Kasbah Souss at your own pace: potters, leatherworkers, silversmiths and weavers, all working by hand.

5. The Feast. You sit down and eat what you cooked — with mint tea, conversation, and the satisfaction of a meal you'll be recreating at home for years.

Cultural Connection
Food is the most honest door into Amazigh culture. The tagine you'll cook is the same dish families across the Souss-Massa region have made for generations — slow, patient, built around what the land and the souk provide. And because your class takes place inside Kasbah Souss, you're surrounded by the other crafts that define this heritage: pottery, leather, silver, and weaving, each one a different language of the same culture. You're not just learning a recipe; you're spending a day inside a living tradition.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need any cooking experience?
None at all. Our chef guides you through every step, and the class is designed to be fun for complete beginners and confident cooks alike.

Can I choose which dish to cook?
Yes — you'll choose between a traditional tagine, couscous, or another Moroccan specialty. Let us know any preferences or dietary requirements when you book.

Is it suitable for children and families?
Absolutely — it's one of our most family-friendly experiences, and kids love the souk walk and getting their hands into the cooking. The family coupon makes it even better value.

What happens if I have dietary restrictions?
Tell us in advance (vegetarian, allergies, etc.) and we'll adapt your ingredients and dish. Moroccan cuisine is naturally flexible.

How long is the whole experience?
Around 5 hours from pickup to drop-off, including the souk visit, cooking class, free time in the crafts complex, and the meal.
