## Overview

Camel trekking has a reputation for being "a tourist thing." Sometimes it is. But a slow, gentle ride through the ancient **Aghroud forest**, past the shimmering **Souss River**, with mint tea and a sizzling BBQ at the end? That's not box-ticking — that's an afternoon your family will talk about for years.

The camels are patient, gentle and frankly a little dramatic about standing up and sitting down — you'll understand the first time. The pace is slow, the shade is cool, and the photos are unreal. And unlike the desert camps two hours inland, we're just **twenty minutes from your hotel** — so you spend your holiday riding, not sitting in a minibus.

## The Experience (Itinerary)

**1. Hotel Pickup.** Comfortable transfer to the Aghroud forest, just south of Agadir.

**2. Meet the Camels.** A gentle introduction to your camel, then we help you up — the famous "wobble" as they stand is all part of the fun.

**3. Forest & River Trek.** Traverse the ancient forest trails, cross the shallow Souss River, and pause for photos at the best river spots.

**4. BBQ & Mint Tea at Kasbat Souss.** We finish with a traditional Moroccan feast — grilled meats, salads, bread and endless mint tea, in the company of local artisans.

## Cultural Connection

The camel is the heartbeat of the desert. For Amazigh families of the Souss-Massa region, these animals carried goods, people and stories across the dunes for centuries. Our trek follows the same forest trails the caravans used, and ends at the **Kasbat Souss** artisan complex — where local craftsmen and women keep Berber traditions alive in leather, pottery and textiles. This is the desert's gentle, authentic side.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is camel trekking suitable for children and older people?**
Yes — this is our gentlest experience. The pace is slow, the terrain is flat, and the camels are calm and well-trained. We've taken guests from 3 to 80 years old.

**Do I need to bring anything special?**
Just comfortable clothes and closed-toe shoes. We provide everything else, including mint tea and a full BBQ at the end.

**How long is the ride itself?**
Around 45 minutes to an hour of riding, with the whole experience (transport, briefing, trek, feast) lasting about 2 hours.

**Is it safe?**
Very. Our camels are gentle and handled by experienced guides, and the forest trails are sheltered and easy. The dramatic standing-up moment is the only surprise — and it's all part of the fun.
