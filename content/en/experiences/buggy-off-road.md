## Overview

If the quad is the fun version, the **buggy is the serious version.** Two seats, a powerful engine, and terrain that would destroy your rental car — and we hand you the wheel. This is our premium offering: two hours of pure off-road exhilaration across majestic sand dunes and desert trails where the only other tracks are ours from the day before.

We only take a small number of buggies out per day — partly for safety, partly because a convoy of ten is nobody's idea of an adventure. The machines are **brand-new and factory-serviced**, and our local guides know every ridge and valley like the back of their hand. You'll come back covered in sand, ears ringing, grinning like a teenager.

## The Experience (Itinerary)

**1. Hotel Pickup.** Comfortable, air-conditioned transfer to our private dune base — a short drive from your hotel.

**2. Briefing & Gear.** Safety briefing, helmet and goggles, then a quick practice lap so you get a feel for the power before the sand opens up.

**3. The Dunes.** Open the throttle across dune fields and desert trails. The climbs are steeper, the drops are faster, and the views over the Atlantic are worth the dust alone.

**4. Desert Trails & Tea.** After the adrenaline, we cool down on the forest trails and finish with mint tea at basecamp, watching the sunset paint the dunes gold.

## Cultural Connection

The dunes we ride are part of the same landscape that has shaped Amazigh life for centuries — the Souss-Massa region, where Berber tribes moved their herds between the desert and the coast. Nearby, the **Kasbat Souss** artisan complex keeps those traditions alive: leather, pottery and metalwork made by hand, exactly as they've been for generations. Your ride is a modern adventure in a land with ancient roots.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a driving licence for the buggy?**
A standard driving licence is required to drive the buggy (any category). Your passenger doesn't need one. No off-road experience necessary — we'll show you everything.

**Can two people ride together?**
Yes — every buggy is a two-seater, and it's the perfect couple or duo activity. Driver and passenger swap whenever you like during the ride.

**Is it safe for beginners?**
Completely. You follow your guide, ride at your pace, and the terrain is wide-open desert with no traffic. Our guides have rescued more rental cars than they care to mention — your buggy is built for this.

**Why is the buggy more expensive than the quad?**
Power, suspension and space. A buggy is a proper off-road vehicle — better on rough terrain, more comfortable, and it takes two people. If adrenaline is the goal, this is the top shelf.
