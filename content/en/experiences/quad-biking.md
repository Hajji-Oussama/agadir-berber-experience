## Overview

Close your eyes for a second. That's the sound of an engine firing up at the edge of the desert — and in about twenty minutes, that engine is **yours to control**.

This is the ride that made Agadir famous on every travel forum worth reading: **two hours of pure, unfiltered adrenaline** as you carve across golden sand dunes, chase the coastline where the Sahara literally kisses the Atlantic, and feel the kind of freedom that a week on a sun lounger simply cannot buy. At **Agadir Berbère Expérience**, we run a small, carefully managed fleet of **top-quality, factory-serviced quad bikes** that we inspect before every single ride. Our guides are **true locals** — born and raised in the Souss-Massa region — who have ridden these trails since childhood. You're not a number in a queue here; you're a guest we're personally responsible for, from the moment we pick you up to the moment we drop you back at your hotel.

## The Experience (Itinerary)

**1. VIP Hotel Pickup.** We collect you directly from your accommodation in Agadir or Taghazout in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle. No hunting for meeting points, no waiting around — just a chilled drive out to our private dune base.

**2. Safety Briefing & Fitting.** We kit you up (helmet, gloves, goggles) and run a friendly, 10-minute briefing on how to handle your quad — throttle, brakes, sand technique. If you've never ridden, you'll feel confident by the end of it. If you have ridden, you'll be grinning at how well these machines handle.

**3. The Desert & Forest Ride.** This is the main event. You'll climb dune faces that look impossible, ride ridgelines with the ocean on your right, and drop into hidden valleys where the only sound is your engine echoing off the sand. Then we cut into the cool shade of the Aghroud forest trails — a side of Agadir 90% of tourists never see.

**4. Authentic Mint Tea Break.** We finish at our basecamp with traditional Moroccan mint tea and a chance to shake the sand out of your boots. It's a small ritual, but honestly? It's the moment you'll remember — engine off, tea in hand, watching the sun drop into the Atlantic.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a driving licence to ride a quad in Agadir?**

No — you don't need any licence or previous experience. Our quads are automatic, and the terrain is open and forgiving. After a short briefing on handling and sand technique, most first-timers are comfortably carving their own tracks within ten minutes. The only requirements are a minimum age of 18 to drive solo and a reasonable level of physical health.

**Is quad biking safe for beginners?**

Yes — and we take that seriously. You're accompanied by an experienced local guide at all times, riding at your pace, never rushed. Every bike is serviced before each ride, you're fully briefed on safety, and the route is chosen based on your experience level. Beginners get the smooth valley trails; confident riders get the dune faces. Either way, the safety equipment — helmet, goggles, gloves — is always included.

**Can children ride a quad in Agadir?**

Absolutely — as passengers. Kids aged **6 and up** can ride comfortably on the back of a quad with a parent or guardian at the controls, and it's genuinely one of the most popular family activities we run. For safety, children need to be tall enough to hold the handlebar grips from the passenger position. We'll always check and advise you before the ride — your child's comfort is our priority.
