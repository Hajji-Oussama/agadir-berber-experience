## Overview

Immerse yourself in the ancient art of Moroccan pottery. At the **Kasbat Souss** artisan complex, we sit you down beside skilled local artisans and put clay in your hands. They'll show you techniques passed down through generations — shaping, smoothing, carving — until your own unique piece takes form.

It's slower, quieter and more personal than any sightseeing stop. You'll create something real, take it home as a lasting memory, then celebrate with traditional mint tea and a sizzling **BBQ feast** in the artisan courtyard. Perfect for families, couples, and anyone who'd rather make a memory than buy one.

## The Experience (Itinerary)

**1. Welcome at Kasbat Souss.** Meet your artisan and settle into the workshop — surrounded by finished pieces that show exactly what's possible.

**2. Hands-On Pottery.** Learn to centre the clay, shape your piece, and carve traditional Amazigh patterns. Our artisans guide every step; no talent required.

**3. Your Piece, Your Memory.** We fire and glaze your creation for you to collect before you leave — a one-of-a-kind souvenir from Agadir.

**4. Mint Tea & BBQ.** Celebrate your masterpiece with a traditional feast, laughter and stories in the courtyard.

## Cultural Connection

Pottery is one of the oldest crafts in the Souss-Massa region — Amazigh artisans have shaped the region's clay for over a thousand years, decorating it with the geometric patterns you'll see on every Berber carpet and tile. The **Kasbat Souss** complex exists to keep these traditions alive, and when you sit at the wheel beside our artisans, you become part of that story — not a spectator, but a maker.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need any artistic ability?**
None. The whole point is that our artisans guide your hands — everyone leaves with a piece they're proud of, no matter their skill level.

**Can children join?**
Absolutely — it's one of our most loved family activities. Kids get a huge kick out of shaping clay, and our artisans are wonderfully patient.

**What happens to my piece?**
We fire and glaze it for you, and it's ready to collect the same visit. It's a genuine, one-of-a-kind Agadir souvenir.

**How long does the whole experience take?**
About 2 hours, including the mint tea and BBQ feast. It's a relaxing half-afternoon, perfect to pair with a morning at the beach.
