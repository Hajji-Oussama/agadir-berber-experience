## Overview

There are a few places on earth where you can ride a horse on the sand with the sun setting over the ocean. Agadir is one of them — and honestly, it never gets old for us either. We take you through the mystical **Aghroud forest** first: cork oaks, sandy trails, and the kind of silence you only find away from the city. Then you break out onto the beach, and the world opens up.

If you've never ridden, our horses are the calm, "I've done this a thousand times" kind. If you have ridden, you'll get to actually move. After the ride, we gather at Kasbat Souss for traditional **mint tea and a BBQ feast** — laughter, stories, and the best meal of your trip.

> "The most romantic thing we did in Morocco. Riding along the empty beach at sunset felt like a movie. The horse was gentle, the guide was wonderful, and the BBQ after was unforgettable." — **Camille R., Lyon, France** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## The Experience (Itinerary)

**1. Hotel Pickup.** We collect you and drive to our ranch at the edge of the Aghroud forest.

**2. Meet Your Horse.** We match each rider to the right horse and run a short, friendly safety briefing. Nervous first-timers get the gentle giants; experienced riders get the lively ones.

**3. Forest & Beach Ride.** Through the cork-oak forest, across the shallow Souss River, then out onto the open Atlantic beach at golden hour. This is the moment.

**4. Mint Tea & BBQ at Kasbat Souss.** We finish with a traditional Moroccan feast — mint tea, grilled meats, and stories around the table.

## Cultural Connection

The Amazigh people have ridden these lands for centuries — horses are woven into the identity of the Souss-Massa region. Riding through the Aghroud forest and finishing at the **Kasbat Souss** artisan complex connects you directly to that heritage: the same forest trails, the same river crossings, and the same hospitality that has welcomed travellers for generations. You're not just on a horse ride; you're following a path our ancestors knew well.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need riding experience?**
No. We match the horse to your level — beginners get calm, experienced horses and simple terrain; experienced riders get the pace they're looking for. A helmet and briefing are always included.

**Is it safe?**
Yes. Our horses are well-trained and calm, guides stay with the group the whole time, and the beach and forest trails are gentle, open terrain. Children ride with a guide or parent.

**What's the best time to ride?**
The golden-hour slot — late afternoon — is spectacular and books out fast. The light over the beach and ocean is the reason this is our most popular couples' activity.

**Can children ride?**
Absolutely. Younger children ride double with an adult or a guide, and older kids ride solo on gentle horses. It's a wonderful family activity.
